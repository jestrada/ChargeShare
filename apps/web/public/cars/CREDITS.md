# Car artwork credits

`silver.webp` and `black.webp` are Tesla Model Y images, background removed and resized
to 480 pixels wide. The traced fallback is CC0 from Wikimedia Commons. The cutouts
were background removed, resized to 960 pixels wide and encoded as WebP. The
fallback was traced with potrace and uses `var(--car-color)`. Shipped artwork
contains no legible license plates or Tesla logos/badges.
